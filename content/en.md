**Associate Professor and Assistant Dean, School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen**

My research focuses on the design and analysis of optimization algorithms for machine learning and scientific computing. My interests include distributed and federated optimization, accelerated and primal-dual methods, and optimization for inverse problems and physics-informed neural networks. ## Research {#research}

A recurring theme in our work is how mathematical structure and computational constraints shape the design of efficient optimization algorithms. The following research directions combine theoretical analysis, algorithm development, and numerical computation.

### Distributed and Federated Optimization

We develop optimization algorithms for learning over networks and across distributed clients. Our work addresses gradient tracking, communication compression, network topology, directed graphs, and partial participation in federated learning. We investigate how these factors affect convergence rates and the trade-offs between computation and communication.

[Slides: Decentralized Optimization](https://mingyan08.github.io/Slides/Decentralized.pdf)

### Physics-Informed Neural Networks

We develop optimization methods and neural network architectures for solving differential equations using physics-informed neural networks (PINNs). Our work includes domain decomposition, residual weighting, and network architectures informed by mathematical analysis. We aim to improve approximation accuracy, training stability, and computational efficiency.

### Primal-Dual and Splitting Algorithms

We study operator-splitting algorithms for structured optimization problems involving smooth functions, nonsmooth regularization, and linear operators. Our work includes PD3O and related primal-dual methods, with an emphasis on convergence analysis and efficient algorithm design. Applications include inverse problems, image processing, and distributed optimization.

[Slides: Primal-Dual Algorithms](https://mingyan08.github.io/Slides/PD3O.pdf)

### Sparse Optimization and Inverse Problems

We study optimization methods for sparse and low-rank models, including compressive sensing, robust principal component analysis, image reconstruction, and denoising. Our work exploits mathematical structure to develop efficient algorithms for inverse problems and signal processing.

### AI for Optimization and Scientific Computing

We explore how machine learning can contribute to the design of optimization algorithms and numerical solvers. Our interests include using neural networks and reinforcement learning to adapt algorithmic parameters and exploit problem-specific structure, particularly in optimization and inverse problems. We seek to combine data-driven approaches with the mathematical principles underlying classical optimization methods.

## Join Us {#join}

We welcome inquiries from prospective PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and visiting students interested in optimization, machine learning, and scientific computing. We value strong mathematical foundations, independent thinking, and an interest in both theoretical analysis and algorithm development.

### PhD Students

Prospective PhD students should have a solid background in mathematics, computer science, data science, or a related field. Familiarity with optimization, numerical methods, machine learning, or scientific computing is particularly valuable.

If you are interested in joining the group, please email me with the subject **PhD Application - [Your Name]** and include your CV, transcript, and a brief statement of research interests. You may also include relevant publications or project reports, if available.


### Postdoctoral Researchers

Researchers with expertise in optimization, numerical analysis, machine learning, or scientific computing are encouraged to contact me. Relevant experience in algorithm design, convergence analysis, or numerical computation is particularly welcome.
If you are interested in joining the group, please email me with the subject **Postdoc Application - [Your Name]** and include your CV, two representative papers, and a brief research statement outlining your previous work and future research interests.

### Visiting Students and Research Interns

We also welcome inquiries from students interested in short-term research visits or internships. Please send your CV, transcript, proposed visit period, and a brief description of your research interests.
